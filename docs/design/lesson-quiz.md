# Lesson steps and the lesson quiz

Steps are where you learn. The lesson quiz is where you show you've learned it, and passing it is what unlocks the lessons that build on it.

## Lesson shape

1. Recap and "What is X?", as now.
2. **1–4 steps**, as many as the topic needs. Each step is explanation, worked example, then questions.
3. **Explain it:** the written question (`final`), with the model answer and self-check, as now. It isn't part of the quiz score: you mark it yourself after seeing the model answer, so it can't be hidden.
4. **The lesson quiz.**
5. Summary.

## Step questions: practice

- **One set of questions**, labelled "Question 1", "Question 2", and so on. The concept question still comes first (it keeps `role: basics` in the content files as its marker), but it's labelled and counted like every other question.
- **Feedback after every answer:** right or wrong, why, and the working.
- **The step is done after 2 right in a row**, counting every question. Questions still go easy to hard and drop back after a miss.
- **You can't fail a step.** There's no 5-question cap and no "Try again later". Questions keep coming until you get 2 in a row. Once a step runs out of questions you haven't seen, it reuses the ones you saw longest ago, and templated questions get new numbers.
- **Practise again:** any finished step can be reopened from the step list for more questions, with feedback. These don't change your progress or XP.
- **XP per step** as now: 10, 9, 8 or 7 for finishing in 2, 3, 4 or 5+ questions.

## The lesson quiz: the check

**Questions.** The quiz is built from the lesson's own step questions, so no new content is needed:

| Steps | Questions per step | Total |
| ----- | ------------------ | ----- |
| 1     | 6                  | 6     |
| 2     | 3                  | 6     |
| 3     | 2                  | 6     |
| 4     | 2                  | 8     |

- Within a step, the questions are spread across difficulties: for 2 questions, one easier and one harder; for 3 or more, as even a spread over 1, 2 and 3 as the step allows.
- The quiz prefers questions you haven't seen in this lesson. Templated questions get new numbers.
- A retake avoids the previous attempt's questions where it can.

**While taking it.**

- "Question 3 of 8": answer, then Next. No right or wrong, no hints, and the tutor is off.
- There's no timer. Answers are saved as you go, so a reload or closing the tab loses nothing.

**Results.**

- **Score and pass/fail.** The pass mark is 80%, which means at most one miss for a 6–8 question quiz.
- **How each step went,** e.g. "Step 2 · The learning rate: 1 of 2 right", with a link to reread it. The results never say which question was wrong or what the right answer was.
- **Passed:** the lesson is complete. You earn 1 XP per question, the first time you pass only. "See answers" then shows every question from that attempt with your answer, the right answer and the working.
- **Not passed yet:** "Retake quiz" gives you new questions straight away. If you leave it, the quiz comes back in your next session.

## Unlocking

- **A lesson is complete when its quiz is passed.**
- **Lessons that build directly on it** (hard prerequisites) stay locked until then, and say "Pass the X quiz first". Everything else stays open, including lessons that list it only as a soft prerequisite.
- **Practice alone doesn't unlock.** A correct step answer can lift a topic to "Familiar", which today unlocks the lessons after it. Once you've started a lesson, only its quiz unlocks them. Topics you placed out of in the diagnostic (no lesson started) still unlock from mastery, as now.
- **Course page:** the new status "Quiz not passed" means every step is done but the quiz isn't passed.

## Reviews and mastery

- Quiz answers are saved as `assessment` attempts and update mastery like any other answer. They count as independent: no hints, first try.
- Spaced reviews are unchanged.

## Your existing progress

- **Lessons you've already completed stay complete.** Their quiz is optional, from the lesson page.
- **Lessons stuck on "Try again later"** resume on the step you were on, with the new rules.
- **Question IDs and answers are unchanged.**

## Content and skill

- **Validator:** a lesson has 1–4 steps. The first question of each step is still a concept multiple-choice question.
- **Skill:** drop the "Basics question" wording, and say the number of steps is whatever the topic needs. Every step needs at least 5 questions across difficulties 1–3, as now. Today's lessons have 5–10 per step, which is enough for practice, the quiz and a few retakes.

## Not in this change

- Mixed quizzes across several lessons (the redesign's quiz every ~150 XP).
- Timed quizzes.
