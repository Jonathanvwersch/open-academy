the lantern keeper opens the gate at dawn. a small boat waits beside the stone wall. the river carries leaves past the quiet garden.
mira puts a blue cup on the table. oren brings warm bread from the kitchen. they watch the rain and listen to the bell in the tower.
at noon the clouds lift above the hill. the keeper walks along the path and counts the boats. each boat has a lamp and a folded map.
when night comes the gate is closed. mira lights the lantern and oren reads a story. outside the river moves under a sky full of stars.
the next morning a bird sings by the window. the bread is warm again. the keeper takes the map and follows the path back to the river.
on market day the garden fills with voices. a child trades a red stone for a little bell. the boats return before the evening rain.
oren asks where the river ends. mira points beyond the hill. we can follow it tomorrow she says. tonight we will keep the lantern bright.
the keeper writes each journey in a book. some pages tell of wind and rain. other pages tell of calm water and the sound of birds.
